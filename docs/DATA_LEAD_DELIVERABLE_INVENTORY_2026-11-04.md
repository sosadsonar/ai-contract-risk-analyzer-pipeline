## Scope and counting rules

This inventory maps the Data Lead (N2) responsibilities in `PROGRESS.md` and `DATA_PLAN.md` to the governing requirements in BRD 0.5 §§7–8 and SRS/TDD evaluation requirements. `PROGRESS.md` assigns N2 the law corpus, retrieval, Data Graph, gold data, and evaluation harness (C-12, C-13, C-18, X-03, X-04, X-10). The assignment table is explicitly marked proposed; shared/group tasks remain shared.

Each row is one independently gated Data Lead deliverable. The working denominator is **19 rows**: nested checks and metric-specific artifacts that share one gold packet or one milestone package are grouped under that acceptance outcome; technical plumbing, admitted data/gold, project evaluation, and final reporting are separate outcomes. A fixture, candidate, queue, or validator proves preparation only; it does not count as accepted data, benchmark evidence, or a human/legal approval. This is an auditable counting model for the user-delegated Data Lead scope, not approval of the proposed role assignments in BRD §9.6; shared approval gates remain explicit blockers.
